PERFORMANCE SYSTEM

Generate, mutate and perform.

TURING SEQUENCERS

Independent generative lanes with density, variation, division, step length, freeze and regeneration.

SYNCED MODULATION

Tempo-locked modulation with independent waveform, rate, target, destination and amount controls.

SOUND ENGINE

Dedicated controls for kick/tom, hats, bass, synth and chords/drone, including performance-oriented synthesis and processing.

LIVE MIXER

Six performance buses, global macros, delay, reverb, beat repeat, synchronized switching, sidechain and temporary snapshots.

BREAK PROCESSOR

Pitch, Roll, Reverse and Chop macros, modular routing, filtering, resonance, drive and tempo-synced effects.

MIDI + RECORDING

Independent Clock In/Out, Notes Out and CC Out, plus stereo master WAV recording.
